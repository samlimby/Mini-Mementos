POCKET MUSIC — FIG-1

Open index.html directly in a browser. No build, dependencies, internet connection,
or server is required. The local preview is http://localhost:3500.

Controls
  Click the casing or surrounding frame / V  Toggle front / isometric view
  Arrow Up / Down  Browse a menu
  Arrow Right / Enter  Select
  Arrow Left  Back
  W  Menu / back
  A / Space  Play or pause
  S  Previous track or restart; hold to rewind
  D  Next track; hold to fast-forward
  Trackpad scroll over the device / drag around the ring  Browse or adjust volume
  Centre button in Now Playing  Switch the wheel between volume and seeking
  Matching keyboard keys depress the on-page keycaps while held.
  Quick taps stay visible briefly; leaving the tab releases every keycap.

Inspection
  3D view / I  Enter inspection mode
  Drag  Rotate freely around the iPod; drag vertically to tilt
  Scroll / + / -  Zoom in or out
  Arrow keys  Rotate (hold Shift for finer steps)
  F / B  Face the front or back toward you
  R  Restore the initial inspection angle and zoom
  Done inspecting  Smoothly settle back to the isometric player view
  Escape / I  Return immediately to the isometric player view
  Grab the iPod during its return to keep inspecting from that angle.
  Music continues during inspection. A / Space still plays or pauses.

Sound begins after pressing play, because browsers require an audio gesture.
Thirteen original electronic instrumentals are synthesized locally with Web Audio.
Browse all 13 under Music > Songs, or the three albums under Music > Albums.
Each track has its own melody, chord progression and arrangement.
Photos contains the original album art. Videos and Podcasts show empty-library states.

Source
  index.html is the complete self-contained deliverable.
  Roboto Mono Regular and Medium are embedded for offline use; its SIL Open Font License
  is included in index.html. The iPod LCD and hardware retain their original styling.
  The interface uses licensed Central Icons, round outlined / radius 2 / stroke 1.5,
  version 1.2.2. Selected SVG sources are in assets/central-icons and embedded
  in index.html. The license key is not stored in the project.
  Text and icons use the Radix UI Gray palette. The page follows the light/dark
  scales; the iPod's LCD, labels and engraving use fixed light-scale Gray tokens.
  audio-engine.js is the editable music engine source, also embedded in index.html.
  If editing that source, replace the AUDIO_ENGINE_START / AUDIO_ENGINE_END block
  in index.html with the updated code.
  The casing uses finely sampled curves, a rounded metal edge profile, and
  blended surface lighting. Bezel lips, shell seams and detailed ports define it.
  The chrome back follows the supplied reference: pale Apple / iPod engraving,
  8GB badge, fine print, regulatory symbols and a rolled lower steel edge.
  inspector-geometry.js is the editable rounded 3D SVG geometry, also embedded
  between INSPECTOR_GEOMETRY_START / INSPECTOR_GEOMETRY_END in index.html.
  ai-iso-skill is the local upstream reference checkout, excluded from Git.
  skills/mini-mementos contains the refined reusable skill shipped here.
  The skill was installed at ~/.claude/skills/mini-mementos as requested.
